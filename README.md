## 🌍 Overview & Global Heritage

The African continent is home to ancient, sacred, and revitalized writing systems that represent over **200 million people** across Eastern, Northern, and Western Africa:

1. **Ge'ez / Ethiopic (ግዕዝ / አማርኛ)**: One of humanity's oldest living abugida scripts, used for over 2,000 years in Ethiopia and Eritrea for Amharic, Tigrinya, and Ethiopian Orthodox liturgy.
2. **Tifinagh (ⵜⵉⴼⵉⵏⴰⵖ)**: The indigenous Berber/Amazigh script of North Africa (Morocco, Algeria, Sahara), officially recognized and taught in national curricula.
3. **N'Ko (ߒߞߏ)**: The indigenous Manding script created by Solomana Kante in 1949, read right-to-left across Guinea, Mali, and Côte d'Ivoire.
4. **Adlam (𞤀𞤣𞤤𞤢𞤥)**: The modern alphabet created in Guinea by Ibrahima and Abdoulaye Barry for the Fulani (Pulaar) language, now supported across global operating systems.
